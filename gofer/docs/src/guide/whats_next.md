# What's Next?

Anything!

1. Make your pipeline [run on its own](../ref/pipeline_configuration/subscriptions.md), on a schedule or when something
   happens on Github.
2. Spruce up your [pipeline definition](../ref/pipeline_configuration/index.html) with
   [task dependencies, secrets, and objects](../ref/pipeline_configuration/tasks.md).
3. Learn what happens when you [update a pipeline](../ref/pipeline_configuration/updating.md).
4. Keep playing with Gofer locally; `gofer --help` lists every command.
5. Learn more about Gofer [terminology](../glossary.md).
6. [Deploy Gofer for real](../ref/server_configuration/index.html). Pair it with your favorite scheduler and start
   using it to automate your jobs.
